Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 30 July 2026

Fresh Fish Web Development ("we", "our" or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website we may collect:

  • Your name

  • Information you include within your enquiry

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide.

2. Why We Collect Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Respond to enquiries

  • Discuss website projects
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve our services

Your information will never be sold to third parties.

3. How Your Information Is Stored

Enquiries submitted through our contact form are securely delivered using EmailJS and received through our business email account. We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorised access.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. Information may only be shared where necessary to operate our services, such as:

  • EmailJS (contact form delivery)
  • Email hosting provider

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain enquiry information only for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry, provide our services, or comply with legal obligations.

6. Cookies

This website currently uses only essential cookies required for normal website functionality. We do not currently use advertising or tracking cookies. If this changes, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

7. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you may have the right to:

  • Request a copy of your personal data
  • Request corrections to inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data where appropriate
  • Object to certain types of data processing
  • Request restriction of processing

8. Third-Party Services

Our website may use trusted third-party services to provide functionality, including EmailJS for contact form delivery. These providers process information only as required to provide their services.

9. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
